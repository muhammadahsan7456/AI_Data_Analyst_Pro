Platform Architecture & User Guide

Welcome to the AI Data Analyst Pro official documentation. This enterprise analytics platform transforms multi-format raw datasets into instant T-SQL intelligence, automated dark-theme visualizations, voice-driven insights, and executive reports in real-time.

1. Multi-Format Dataset Uploader

The uploader automatically detects file encodings, cleans header column names, and provisions an isolated physical table inside SQL Server.

Supported Extensions & Types:
  • CSV (.csv) & TSV (.tsv) — Auto-detected delimiters
  • Excel (.xlsx, .xls) — Multi-sheet support
  • JSON (.json) & XML (.xml) — Hierarchical flattening
  • Parquet (.parquet) & Feather (.feather) — High-speed columnar data
  • Text (.txt) — Fixed-width & delimited text files

2. Voice AI & Fast Query Engine

Ask questions using voice or text in natural English or Roman Urdu. The engine normalizes speech typos and executes queries in sub-second time.

Query Syntax & Voice Examples:
  • "top 10 rows" or "top 5 ross" ➔ Instant T-SQL Top N
  • "mujhe sales ka data dikhao" ➔ Column Search & Aggregation
  • "Count total records" ➔ SELECT COUNT(*) FROM [...]
  • "Show top customers by revenue" ➔ Grouped T-SQL aggregation

3. Enterprise Dark Visualizations

Every analytical response automatically generates high-resolution enterprise chart visualizations with numerical callout values.

Chart Types Supported:
  • Bar & Column Charts — Categorical distributions & Top N
  • Line & Area Charts — Trend analysis & temporal patterns
  • Pie & Donut Charts — Proportional share breakdowns
  • Scatter Plots & Histograms — Distribution & correlation

4. Full Export Center

Download complete dataset records and generated reports across multiple executive document formats with full embedded charts.

Available Formats:
  • Excel (.xlsx) — Clean formatted sheets with summary KPI tab
  • PDF Report — Executive layout with AI insights & high-res chart
  • Word (.docx) & PowerPoint (.pptx) — Slide decks & docs
  • Vector SVG (.svg) & CSV (.csv) — Raw data streams
