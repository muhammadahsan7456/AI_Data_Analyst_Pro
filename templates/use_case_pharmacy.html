Pharmacy Sales & Medicine Inventory Analytics

Running a pharmacy or chain of medical stores requires razor-sharp inventory control. Avoid expired drug losses, ensure critical life-saving antibiotics are never out of stock, and audit distributor purchase discounts with AI.

Near-Expiry Stock Warnings

Filter medicine batches expiring within 30, 60, or 90 days. Return slow-moving lots to pharmaceutical distributors before expiry write-offs occur.

Fast-Moving vs. Slow-Moving Drugs

Identify high-turnover medicines (e.g. analgesics, anti-hypertensives) and calculate your optimum reorder point based on historical sales velocity.

Distributor Margin Auditing

Compare purchase invoices across pharmaceutical distributors. Discover variance in distributor bonus schemes and trade discounts.

Doctor Prescription Trends

Analyze prescription origin sheets to understand which clinics and specialists refer the highest volume of high-value treatments to your store.
