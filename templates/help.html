How Can We Help You Today?

Find instant answers to technical questions, troubleshooting tips, voice query syntax, and export tutorials.

What dataset file formats are supported?
AI Data Analyst Pro supports 9 enterprise formats up to 100MB per file:
  • CSV (.csv) & TSV (.tsv) (Comma and tab delimited)
  • Excel (.xlsx, .xls) (Multi-sheet workbooks)
  • JSON (.json) & XML (.xml) (Nested hierarchical datasets)
  • Parquet (.parquet) & Feather (.feather) (Columnar binary storage)
  • Text (.txt) (Fixed width and delimited text files)
How does the AI Voice Agent work?
Click the "Voice AI" microphone button on the query page. The Web Speech API transcribes your speech in real-time in English or Roman Urdu. The system auto-corrects speech typos (such as "top 5 ross" ➔ "top 5 rows" or Urdu numbers "paanch" ➔ "5") and runs the query in sub-second time (0.02s) without reloading the page.
Why does the AI refuse casual/general knowledge questions?
The platform enforces strict AI Context Guardrails. Queries unrelated to uploaded datasets (such as "who is president" or "tell me a joke") are intercepted in 0.001s without touching the database, saving processing resources and protecting enterprise data focus.
What happens when I delete a dataset?
When you delete a dataset, the system executes a DROP TABLE [{table_name}] command in SQL Server, cleans up associated query logs, and deletes the metadata row. The data is permanently purged and cannot be recovered.
In what formats can I export my reports?
You can export reports in 6 enterprise formats in sub-second time: PowerPoint (.pptx), Executive PDF (with high-res charts), Excel (.xlsx) (with summary KPI sheet), Word (.docx), Vector SVG (.svg), and CSV (.csv).
